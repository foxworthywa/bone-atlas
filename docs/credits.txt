Changes in this edition: selected 222 bone/cartilage meshes; decompressed geometry into JSON; applied source translations, rotations and scales (including reflected winding); simplified metadata; replaced materials; added course-specific labels, draft point markers, review controls and camera presets. The original six prototype meshes retain their coordinates and IDs so instructor annotations remain compatible. Adapted model geometry and supplied model annotations remain CC BY-SA 4.0. The published annotation set incorporates 142 retained markers reviewed by the course instructor and supplied in the September 13, 2026 export. Composite joint, hard palate and subpubic angle targets were removed; nuchal lines and the rib-tubercle costal facet target were removed per instructor notes. Olfactory foramina are explained within the cribriform plate entry.

Course structure names and scope are based on the supplied Axial List and Appendicular List for BIO 141. The catalogue contains 235 entries, including grouped and individual digit targets. Points represent locations, not complete structure boundaries. Frontal sinus and interosseous membranes are recorded as unavailable in this geometry. Other small openings and internal features require instructor assessment of model detail.
